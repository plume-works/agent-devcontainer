# 🔬 Findings

*Measurements and investigations, one per document in `findings/<slug>.md`.
Frontmatter is `type: finding`. A finding states what was measured, each
hypothesis eliminated and what eliminated it, what would settle it, and a
`## Closing` that says whether it is open. A decision a finding settles goes
into the owning spec's `## Rationale`, and the finding links that spec.*
